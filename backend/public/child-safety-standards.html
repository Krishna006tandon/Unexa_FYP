UNEXA - Child Safety Standards Policy

Application: UNEXA

Developer: Krishna Tandon

Last Updated: October 02, 2026

Zero Tolerance Policy: UNEXA strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE) in any form. We are committed to maintaining a safe environment and upholding the highest child protection standards across our application.

1. Prohibition of CSAE and CSAM

UNEXA strictly bans:

  • Any production, transmission, uploading, sharing, or storage of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Any grooming, solicitation, or sexual exploitation targeting minors.
  • Any visual, audio, or textual content depicting or facilitating child exploitation.

2. Immediate Enforcement & Account Termination

When any content or activity violating this policy is identified:

  • Immediate Removal: All offending material is immediately and permanently removed from our servers.
  • Permanent Account Ban: The user account responsible is permanently terminated with no possibility of reinstatement.
  • IP and Device Block: We actively ban the associated IP and device identifiers to prevent re-registration.

3. Mandatory Reporting to Law Enforcement & NCMEC

In full compliance with applicable international, federal, and local laws, UNEXA promptly reports any detected instances of CSAM or child exploitation to:

  • The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via official reporting portals.
  • Relevant regional, national, and international law enforcement agencies.

4. How Users Can Report Child Safety Concerns

Users who encounter any concerning content, behavior, or potential child safety violations should report it immediately:

  • By Direct Priority Email: Send an immediate report to our designated child safety team at krishna.a.tandon@gmail.com with the subject line URGENT: Child Safety Report. Include the reported username, date/time, and relevant details.
  • All reports are reviewed within 24 hours with highest priority.
