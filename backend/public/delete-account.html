UNEXA - Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: UNEXA

Developer: Krishna Tandon

At UNEXA, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Follow these steps to submit a deletion request:

  1. Send an email from your registered UNEXA email address to: krishna.a.tandon@gmail.com.
  2. Set the email subject to: Request Account Deletion - UNEXA.
  3. In the email body, include your Username and confirm that you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon verifying your request, we permanently purge the following data from our active databases and servers:

  • Account Credentials & Profile: Username, Email address, Profile photo, and Bio.
  • Personal Communications: All 1-on-1 and group chat messages sent by your account.
  • User-Generated Media: Uploaded images, voice recordings, and video clips stored on cloud storage.
  • Social Connections: Followers, Following, and Close Friends lists.

Data Retention Period

All deletion requests are verified and processed within 7 business days. Once deleted, this action is permanent and cannot be undone. Residual backup copies are completely erased according to our routine 30-day backup rotation cycle.

Need Assistance?

If you have any questions or require help regarding your account or data deletion, please contact us at: krishna.a.tandon@gmail.com.
